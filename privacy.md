# Privacy Policy – Resume Bot

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Resume Bot is a personal tool created and used only by its owner, Adheeb Ashim. It is not offered to the public.

## What the app does
The app uses the Gmail API only to send job application emails, with a resume PDF attached, from the owner's own Gmail account.

## Google permissions
The app requests one Google permission: "Send email on your behalf" (the gmail.send scope). It does not read, search, delete or modify any emails.

## Data collection and use
The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data from any user. The only information it handles is the job details and recipient email address the owner enters, and these are used only to prepare and send the application email.

## Data sharing
No data is shared with third parties, except that emails are delivered through Google's Gmail service.

## Contact
For questions about this policy, contact the owner, Adheeb Ashim, at the email address listed as the support email on this app's Google consent screen.
